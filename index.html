What We Take Care Of

CONCRETE

Driveway & Concrete Cleaning

Oil stains, tire marks, leaf tannin, moss between pavers. We pre-treat with the proper solution before washing and run a surface cleaner for even coverage. No tiger striping, no lines.

EXTERIOR

House Washing Richmond BC

Vinyl, stucco, HardiePlank, cedar. Each needs different chemistry and pressure. We match the method to the surface. No streaks, no blotching, no damage from a pressure setting dialled too high for what you have.

Learn more →
GUTTERS

Gutter Cleaning Richmond BC

Richmond gets around 1,150mm of rain a year. Clogged gutters in that climate aren't a minor inconvenience — they back up, overflow, and rot fascia boards. We clear gutters and downspouts and flush the system to confirm it drains properly.

Learn more →
ROOF

Roof Soft Wash & Moss Removal

Richmond roofs moss up fast. Humidity, rainfall, and tree cover mean most homes need attention every two to three years. We use low-pressure soft wash, what shingle manufacturers actually specify, so the moss dies at the root and your warranty stays intact.
