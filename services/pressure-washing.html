Driveway & Concrete Pressure Washing — Richmond BC

Pressure Washing for Hard Surfaces

Concrete, paving stone, and exposed aggregate are designed to take high-pressure cleaning — unlike roofs and siding. But pressure washing done wrong still causes damage: tiger striping from uneven coverage, surface etching from excessive PSI on softer stone, and lines from a single-point nozzle moved too slowly.

We pre-treat surfaces before washing. Oil stains, tire marks, leaf tannin, and moss each require different chemistry to release from the substrate — water pressure alone won't pull an oil stain out of porous concrete. We apply the appropriate pre-treatment, allow dwell time, then run a surface cleaner for even, stripe-free coverage.

Surfaces We Pressure Wash

  • Driveways — poured concrete, exposed aggregate, paving stone, asphalt
  • Walkways and steps — concrete, flagstone, brick
  • Patios and deck boards — concrete and composite; wood decks assessed case by case
  • Garage floors — oil and grease pre-treatment before wash
  • Retaining walls — moss and algae removal from concrete block and stone

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does driveway pressure washing cost in Richmond?
Most residential driveways run $150–$350 depending on size and stain load. Add-ons like pre-treatment for heavy oil staining are quoted separately. Free estimates available.
Can pressure washing remove oil stains from concrete?
Partially, depending on how long the stain has set. Fresh oil (under 6 months) responds well to degreaser pre-treatment followed by hot-water pressure washing. Old, deeply set stains lighten significantly but may not fully disappear.
How often should I pressure wash my driveway in Richmond?
Once per year keeps most Richmond driveways clear of moss, tannin staining from leaves, and surface algae. High-traffic driveways or those under heavy tree cover benefit from cleaning every spring.
