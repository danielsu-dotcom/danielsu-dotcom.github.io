Gutter Cleaning — Richmond BC

Professional Gutter Cleaning and Downspout Flush

⚠️ Did you know falls from ladders are among the most common causes of homeowner injuries in BC?

Richmond receives around 1,150mm of rain per year — one of the highest in Metro Vancouver. In that climate, clogged gutters aren't a minor inconvenience. They back up, overflow against fascia boards, and introduce persistent moisture to the roofline structure. Fascia rot from overflowing gutters is one of the most common and preventable sources of exterior damage on Richmond homes.

We clear gutters and downspouts completely, then flush the system with water to confirm it drains properly before we leave. If a downspout is blocked below grade, we identify it rather than leaving it as a hidden problem.

What the Service Includes

  • Full debris removal from gutters — leaves, moss, seed pods, shingle granule buildup
  • Downspout flush to confirm clear drainage from gutter to exit
  • Visual inspection of gutter hangers, seams, and end caps for any obvious damage
  • Debris bagged and removed — nothing left on the ground

When to Book

Twice per year covers most Richmond properties: once in late autumn after leaf fall (November), and once in spring after blossom and seed drop (April–May). Properties with large deciduous trees overhanging the roofline benefit from an additional check in mid-winter.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does gutter cleaning cost in Richmond?
Most single-storey homes run $120–$200. Two-storey homes typically run $180–$280 depending on linear footage and debris load. Free quotes on request.
How do I know if my gutters are clogged?
Water overflowing at the gutter edge during rain, staining on fascia boards, or plants growing from the gutter are the most common signs. If you haven't had them cleared in over a year in Richmond, they need clearing.
Do you clean the outside of the gutters too?
On request, yes. Black oxidation staining on gutter faces can be removed with soft wash — we treat this as an add-on to the standard service.
